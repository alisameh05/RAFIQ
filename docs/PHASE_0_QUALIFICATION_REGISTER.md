Provider and resource names appear here; credentials, account identifiers, private recipient details and precise locations stay in the approved operations store.

Day 1 status: five of twenty dependency lines have account-level evidence from the 26 November validation pass, summarized in the [validation access evidence](PHASE_0_ACCESS_EVIDENCE.md); none is fully qualified for its production gate. No controlled domain, production identity, official callback, study reservation or paid-risk provider approval is recorded. The [Day 2 escalation record](PHASE_0_DAY_2_ESCALATION.md) carries every unmet prerequisite with its schedule impact.

**Status vocabulary** — **Passed**: evidence recorded and accepted for the named gate. **Pending**: request, access or evidence is outstanding. **Failed**: tested and does not meet the gate. A Pending line keeps its capability gated.
