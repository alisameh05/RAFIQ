## Consent, supervision and data access

The study coordinator obtains and records consent before enrolling a participant. The institutional approver records the applicable procedure decision, including permitted collection and use, setting, approved store, role grants, consent and withdrawal handling, retention and disposal. Record the decision and form versions before collection. Resolve benchmark and journey participation separately where their purposes or approval terms differ.

Stage benchmark examples of unsafe behavior while stationary or in a simulator. The institution approves and names a supervised-use procedure owner before any motion testing. In approved motion tests, use passive driver presentation. The driver authorizes the designated supervisor while stationary. The supervisor can acknowledge warnings, cancel pending contact alerts and pause monitoring. Delegation ends on revocation or session end. Driver setup, starting, resuming, ending and account or record review happen while stationary. Omit motion interactions that require supervision when authorized coverage is unavailable. Use synthetic alerts or approved test recipients where alert checks are in scope. ## RAFIQ Controlled Annotation Guide v0.1

Benchmark eligibility is limited to the versioned, consented stationary or simulator dataset collected for RAFIQ. Use the observable boundaries in the FR-05 behavior catalog for drowsiness, distraction, active phone use, eating and drinking. Evaluate prolonged or slow blinks and repeated yawning within drowsiness. Use talking and isolated yawns as contrast cases. Stage ordinary observations, distinct positives, recurring and overlapping behaviors, occluded eyes or hands, and camera loss. Treat reaching as a separate candidate subject to approval of its label and pass targets. [FR-05 behavior catalog](PRD.md#52-on-device-monitoring-and-warnings) · [Benchmark rules](PRD.md#83-benchmark-and-behavior-policy) · [TRD acceptance targets](TRD.md#8-measurable-acceptance-and-release-gates)

1. **Before collection.** Ali Sameh signs the protocol, tested setup, target thresholds and catalog/annotation versions for the sign-off run.
2. **Before tuning.** The study coordinator and vision owner assign each coded driver to one split and record the dataset manifest. Target 60% development, 20% calibration and 20% held-out by driver. Verify disjoint membership, record actual counts and keep held-out data out of tuning. Reserve at least **five held-out drivers, 20 independently staged positive episodes per behavior and five held-out monitored hours**. Use distinct staged episode IDs to count positives. Multiple labeled frames in one episode count as one episode for this minimum.
3. On the shared 10 Hz timeline, the primary annotator records a label for each evaluated behavior at each sampled timestamp. Use **present** when the positive cue defined by the catalog is observed, **absent** when the cue can be assessed and the behavior is absent, and **unusable** when the required cue cannot be assessed. Label concurrent behaviors independently. Mark all behaviors unusable when the feed is unavailable. Record episode IDs and timing, setup, pause or loss windows, unusable reasons and ambiguous intervals.
4. Before label freeze, a second reviewer independently labels the ambiguous intervals against the same catalog and guide. Record the initial labels, disagreements, final decision and rationale in the restricted review log. The vision owner and reviewer reconcile disagreements before signing the label revision. If visibility prevents a decision, mark the affected class unusable. Keep unresolved disagreements open and hold the affected label freeze. Annotators and reviewers use the source material and guide independently of model predictions.
5. **Before held-out scoring.** The vision owner records the approved PRD revision and the frozen dataset, label, catalog and guide versions, the exact model/assets and runtime, and the calibrated class thresholds, warning timing and cue availability rules. Freeze the 10 Hz matching rules, metric denominators, treatment of unusable intervals, scoring implementation and tested setup. Select the lowest score threshold meeting both per-class calibration targets. Resolve any unmet calibration target before scoring. The independent reviewer verifies the evidence and Ali Sameh approves the freeze. A material change requires a recorded revision and renewed approval and evaluation of affected evidence under the [PRD sign-off rules](PRD.md#7-success-and-release-evidence).

Match labels and predictions at the same sampled timestamps. Exclude unusable frames from that class's confusion counts and report their share. Use elapsed monitoring time excluding deliberate pauses and including observation-loss pauses for coverage. Report usable feed coverage overall and cue coverage by class for each supported browser and device setup and evaluated condition. Use the frozen exposure rule for false warnings per monitored hour. Specify before scoring how uncertain intervals affect the warning count and monitored-hours denominator. Count a combined warning once as false when none of its named classes matches a qualifying labeled episode. Measure delay from labeled dwell qualification to visible warning. Report per-class confusion counts, precision, recall, F1, accuracy and coverage, plus overall coverage, false warnings per hour and the delay distribution. Targets are precision/recall of 90%/90% for drowsiness, 85%/85% for distraction, and 85%/80% each for active phone use, eating and drinking. The other targets are at most two false warnings per monitored hour, p95 warning delay at most 750 ms, and at least 90% overall coverage under supported normal conditions. Report degraded fixtures separately. [Approved scoring baseline](PRD.md#83-benchmark-and-behavior-policy)

## Seven-day journey and hosted availability

Before either window starts, Ali Sameh approves and freezes its protocol, pass thresholds, candidate and tested setup. Record the approved revisions in the worksheets.

Reserve one slot each for **12 unique consenting drivers** over the same seven-day journey window. Schedule an operator for each slot and an independent reviewer for reconciliation and sign-off. Use the standard public self-service path, including an unaffiliated driver without an enrollment record. Keep recovery verification in its own evidence record. The operator records an attempt when the participant begins public signup and checks that the participant completes email verification, camera readiness (D02), start and finish (D03), and reaches a durable summary unassisted. If D05 reports pending data, verify that the participant reaches the ready summary when the required data is durable. Count signup, camera, workflow and sync failures as failed attempts. Target **at least 10 of 12** complete attempts.
